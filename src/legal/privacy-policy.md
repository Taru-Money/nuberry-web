## 7. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

The Platform facilitates investments in mutual funds in the name of a minor through a parent or lawful guardian. Accordingly, we may process personal data relating to minors in connection with such investment, KYC, account administration, transaction facilitation and related services. The parent or lawful guardian is responsible for providing the required information and completing the applicable KYC, verification and consent processes on behalf of the minor, to the extent required under applicable law.

We may collect and process information relating to the minor, including the minor's name, date of birth, PAN where applicable, relationship with the parent or lawful guardian, proof of date of birth, KYC information and documents and other information required by applicable law, the relevant AMC, RTA, KRA or other authorised intermediary for establishing and servicing the investment relationship.

The child-facing application is linked to the parent or lawful guardian’s account. The minor is not required to independently provide personal details through the child-facing application. Information relating to lessons completed, learning progress, difficulty levels and similar educational engagement information may be processed solely to the extent necessary to provide, administer and improve the financial literacy and educational experience and to enable the parent or lawful guardian to view such progress. A parent or lawful guardian may request access to, correction of or erasure of the minor’s personal data, subject to reasonable verification of identity and authority and applicable law.

**7.1 Restrictions on Processing Children’s Personal Data:** The Company shall not undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring of children, or process children’s personal data for targeted advertising, except to the extent expressly permitted under applicable law. Educational engagement information, including lessons completed, learning progress and difficulty levels, may be recorded and processed solely to the extent necessary to provide, administer and improve the relevant educational functionality and enable the parent or lawful guardian to view such progress. Such information shall not be used for unrelated commercial profiling or targeted advertising. Nothing in this provision shall be construed as permitting any tracking, behavioural monitoring or profiling prohibited under applicable law.

We will obtain and maintain verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian before processing personal data of a child where such consent is required under applicable law. Where personal data is collected or processed to comply with KYC, mutual fund, regulatory or other legal requirements, such processing will be carried out in accordance with the applicable legal and regulatory requirements and any consent or other lawful basis required under applicable law. We will implement the safeguards applicable to the processing of children's personal data under applicable law.

**7.2 Deletion of Children’s Educational Data:** Where a parent or lawful guardian closes a child-learning account, withdraws consent where applicable, or requests erasure of the child’s educational engagement information, the Company shall cease the relevant processing where required under applicable law and delete or anonymise such information, as appropriate, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and legitimate retention requirements. Where retention is required under applicable law, the relevant information shall be retained only for the period and purposes permitted or required by law and shall not be used for unrelated purposes.

**7.3 Attainment of Majority**: Upon an individual attaining eighteen (18) years of age, the Company shall, where required under applicable law, obtain the individual’s own consent for continued processing of personal data previously processed on the basis of parental or lawful guardian consent. The processing of that individual’s personal data shall thereafter be governed by applicable law and the individual’s rights under applicable law. The parent’s or lawful guardian’s access to the individual’s personal data and account information through the Platform shall be discontinued or modified as required under applicable law and the applicable account and mutual fund procedures.
